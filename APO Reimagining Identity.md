# APO – Reimagining Identity for Africa

## 🔐 What Is APO?

**APO**, powered by **sui**, is a self-sovereign identity platform designed to simplify, secure, and decentralize identity management in Nigeria and across Africa.

We’re starting as a **CV builder**—a user-friendly, low-friction way to onboard users and collect verified bio-data. But our vision is much bigger: to become the trusted identity layer for education, employment, healthcare, finance, and more.

This is *not* another ID database. This is **“Sign in with Google” meets African identity sovereignty**—built from the ground up for user control, trust, and scale.

---

## 🧩 Core Vision

We believe identity should be:
- **Reusable** – You shouldn’t fill out the same bio-data 50 times.
- **Verifiable** – Institutions should trust what they’re seeing.
- **User-Controlled** – You choose *who* gets *what*, and *when*.
- **Composable** – Credentials should stack across life stages.
- **Consent-Based** – No surveillance, no surprises, no shadow sharing.

We’re building a future where your **UID** is your passport to opportunity—locally and globally.

---

## 🚀 Project Phases

### ✅ Phase 1: CV Builder (Live)
- Build a professional CV in minutes
- Autofill job forms via browser extension
- Issue a shareable UID-linked profile page

### 🧾 Phase 2: Universal Identity System
- One-tap authentication: “Sign in with APO”
- Verifiable records from partners (NIN, WAEC, BVN, JAMB)
- Full control over what data is shared, per use case

### 📊 Phase 3: Data Aggregator (Opt-in)
- Anonymized, user-consented insights for partnerships (e.g., health → nutrition, education → hiring)
- Users are rewarded or empowered—not exploited

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. **User Onboards via CV Builder**
2. **UID Issued with Encrypted Data Fingerprints**
3. **Institutions Request Specific Data via API**
4. **User Approves/Demands Consent**
5. **Audit Trail Logged on Blockchain**
